The Arban legacy

One standard: no plot is sold until it is proven

Arban Properties was founded by Mukiza Arban with a simple vision: make buying land in Uganda safe, affordable and transparent for ordinary families and for investors abroad.

"Land is a family's legacy. It should never be a gamble."
Mukiza Arban, Founder and CEO

We are committed to eliminating real estate fraud through rigorous property inspections and strict legal compliance. Every file we handle follows the same path:

  • Official search at the registry before any money moves.
  • Site inspection of boundaries, occupants, neighbours and access.
  • Title check for the real owners, tenure, caveats and mortgages. Where land is jointly owned, both owners sign.
  • Written agreements drafted for advocate review, with witnesses.
  • Open pricing. Government fees are shown separately and paid at cost.
